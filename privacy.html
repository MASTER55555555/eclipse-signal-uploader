Privacy Policy — Eclipse Signal Uploader

Effective date: 6 October 2026

Who we are

Eclipse Signal Uploader ("the tool") is operated by Onurhan Demir, an individual developer. Contact: onur38740@gmail.com.

Use of YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

Through Google OAuth 2.0, with the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube, the tool accesses only the YouTube channels owned by the authorizing Google account in order to upload videos, set video metadata and privacy status, and read the details of videos it has uploaded. It does not read comments, subscribers, analytics, or any data belonging to other users or channels.

What data is stored and where

The tool stores the OAuth access and refresh tokens and the identifiers of uploaded videos in local files on the operator's own computer. No data is sent to or stored on any server other than Google's. No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

Data retention and deletion

Stored tokens and video identifiers are kept only as long as the tool is in use and are deleted by removing the local files. Identifiers of videos uploaded by the tool are retained as authorized data for as long as needed to prevent duplicate uploads; no other YouTube API data is stored for more than 30 days, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation immediately invalidates the stored tokens; the local files can then be deleted.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
